# JA21 JA Security Policy Language — Twelve-Week Curriculum

| Week | Theme | Chapters | Laboratory evidence |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Suite identity, modules, and policy context | 1–2 | Minimal policy suite and evidence map |
| 2 | Identity, groups, roles, resources, and scope | 3–4 | Identity/resource authorization graph |
| 3 | Capabilities and decision rules | 5–6 | Allow/deny/require decision traces |
| 4 | Composition, conflict, explanation, and approval | 7–8 | Conflict proof and approval packet |
| 5 | Delegation, attenuation, revocation, and expiry | 9–10 | Lifecycle and monotonicity tests |
| 6 | Agent, tool, model, and secret policies | 11–12 | Access and redaction audit |
| 7 | Network/process sandboxes and data lifecycle | 13–14 | Sandbox and retention/deletion tests |
| 8 | Audit, fail-closed behavior, and recovery | 15–16 | Immutable receipt and failure simulation |
| 9 | Compiler frontend and static proof | 17–18 | Typed AST and proof bundle |
| 10 | Runtime enforcement and replay | 19 | Deterministic decision transaction |
| 11 | Optimization, R12, MCRT, and certification | 20–21 | Equivalence and evidence packet |
| 12 | Standalone toolchain capstone | 22 | Auditable offline policy release |

## Assessment

- Laboratories: 40%
- Negative/security/recovery portfolio: 20%
- Mid-course architecture review: 15%
- Capstone suite and evidence packet: 25%
