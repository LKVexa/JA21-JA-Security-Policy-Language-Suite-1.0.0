# Instructor Guide

Teach JA Security as a language of bounded authority and evidence-bearing decisions. Grade explicit identity, scope, precedence, proof, lifecycle, sandbox, redaction, audit, and replay—not merely whether an allow rule exists.

## Five-role review

- SOPHIA: semantic and systemic coherence
- CHARLOTTE: legible policy architecture and explanations
- LANDON: compiler, proof, runtime, sandbox, and replay feasibility
- Professor: invariants, counterexamples, and tests
- Podium: publication, integrity, limitations, and certification

## Rubric

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Source and language correctness | 20% |
| Identity, rule, capability, and scope correctness | 20% |
| Proof, lifecycle, and sandbox correctness | 20% |
| Evidence identity, audit, and replay | 20% |
| Diagnostics, limitations, and publication | 20% |
